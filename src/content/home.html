05 / A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before your
first line.

What should I start with?

A clear architectural sketch or concept image. Make the main forms and perspective legible, then describe the materials, light and atmosphere you want to explore.

Is a visualization a technical drawing?

No. Treat generated imagery as a visual interpretation for concept exploration and discussion. Review it against your source; do not use it as verified dimensions or construction documentation.

Are these images made with ArchLab?

The workflow, comparison and lead gallery image use an existing sketch and visualization pair from the ArchLab project. The animated hero and two supporting gallery studies use AI-generated concept media. Those concept studies are not ArchLab outputs, and the animation is not a product recording.

How many images can I create?

That depends on your plan’s allowance and the credit cost of each operation. The example above explains the calculation. See the plans above for monthly allowances and check the app for operation costs.

Where can I check commercial use and privacy?

Read the terms of service and privacy policy before uploading confidential work or using outputs commercially. Contact ArchLab if your project needs clarification.

How do I manage my subscription?

Open your ArchLab account to find the available subscription controls. For cancellation terms or help with an account, use the contact page.
